Privacy Policy TECNOLINE Kelime Yolculuğu · Last updated: October 29, 2026 This policy explains how the TECNOLINE Kelime Yolculuğu Android app handles data. In brief: The game has no account sign-in or AdMob/ad SDK. Game progress and points are stored on your device and are not sent to TECNOLINE servers. What data is processed? The app stores game state such as unlocked levels, completed levels, points, and progress in app-specific local storage on your device. This is used to remember your progress. The app does not ask you to provide your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information. Is data shared? In this version, game progress is not transmitted to TECNOLINE or another company, sold, or shared for advertising. AdMob has not been added; the app does not display ads or create advertising profiles. Retention and deletion Game progress remains on your device. It is deleted when you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Automatic app-data backup is disabled; device-to-device transfer behavior may vary by manufacturer. No server-side copy of game progress is kept. Security Local game records are kept in Android app-specific storage. Protecting your device lock and keeping Android security updates current also helps protect data on your device. Children's privacy The app does not request personal information and does not send game progress off the device. The app's target-audience settings are declared separately and accurately in Google Play Console. Changes to this policy This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. In particular, the policy and Google Play declarations will be updated before adding advertising, analytics, online sync, or new permissions.
